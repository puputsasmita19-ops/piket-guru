Nama Koleksi Fungsi Utama Field Kunci (*Key Attributes*) Indeks / Relasi
settings Konfigurasi sekolah & geofence schoolName, schoolLat, schoolLng, allowedRadiusMeters Dokumen tunggal school_config
users Kredensial & hak izin peran nip, fullName, role, pinHash, pinSalt, isActive Indexed by nip
teachers Master dewan guru pengajar nip, fullName, mataPelajaran, statusKepegawaian Foreign Key: userId
staff Master pegawai kependidikan nip, fullName, divisi, jabatan Foreign Key: userId
rooms Pos piket & ruangan pantau code, name, building, floor, isActive Primary: id

attendance Presensi GPS & swafoto wajah scheduleId, userId, latitude, longitude, distance, photoUrl Index: tanggal, userId
dutyBooks Jurnal buku piket digital scheduleId, petugasId, 5 kondisi sekolah, catatanPiket, status Index: tanggal, status
incidents Catatan kejadian & kronologi tanggal, waktu, pelaporId, lokasi, kategori, status Index: tanggal, status
